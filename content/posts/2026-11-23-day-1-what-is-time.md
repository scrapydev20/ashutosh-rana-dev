Day 1 of thinking about time, and the rabbit hole is already getting deeper. After learning how physical clocks work and how oscillators make them tick, a much harder question hit me: what is time, actually? My first instinct was simple. It's the ticking of a clock, the seconds, minutes, hours, days, and centuries passing by. Wait, no. Those are just units we use to measure time; they aren't time itself. So what exactly are we measuring when we say time passes?
